About RouterFleet Improved

RouterFleet Improved

by Alessio Bravi · version {{ routerfleet_version }}

RouterFleet Improved by Alessio Bravi is an open-source, self-hosted platform for managing a fleet of network devices.

It backs up every device's configuration on a schedule and keeps its history, with a line-by-line comparison between any two versions; opens a console on any device right in the browser, over SSH or Telnet; runs commands across many devices at once and checks that they had the intended effect; watches whether devices are reachable and notifies you when they are not; and records who signed in and who changed what.

Supported devices
  • MikroTik (RouterOS, including branded RouterOS devices)
  • OpenWrt
  • Ubiquiti (airOS)

Support for more devices is welcome: request it with the button below, describing the device and how it can be backed up.

Where it comes from

RouterFleet Improved started from, and was inspired by, the earlier RouterFleet projects:

  • RouterFleet, the original project, created by Eduardo Garcia da Silva, with contributions by petrunetworking;
  • RouterFleet Extended by Dustin Thal, which built on it.

Their copyright notices are kept in the MIT License, and databases from both projects upgrade to RouterFleet Improved in place.
